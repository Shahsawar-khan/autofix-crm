# AutoFix Workshop CRM - Client Handover Guide

Welcome to your new Sales CRM! This guide will help your workshop manager get started quickly.

## 🚀 How to Access the CRM
Once deployed, open your browser and go to:
- **Dashboard:** `http://your-domain.com/dashboard` (Overview of revenue, jobs, and reminders)
- **Pipeline Board:** `http://your-domain.com/pipeline` (Drag and drop vehicles)
- **Add New Lead:** `http://your-domain.com/leads/create`
- **Follow-up Reminders:** `http://your-domain.com/reminders`
- **Email Logs:** `http://your-domain.com/emails`

## 🛠️ How to Manage a Customer (The Workflow)

**Step 1: A New Customer Arrives**
When a customer walks in, click the green **"+ Add New Lead"** button on the Pipeline Board. Fill in their name, phone, car details, and the service needed (e.g., Brake Pad Replacement).

**Step 2: Track Progress via Pipeline**
The car will appear in the **"NEW"** column. As the work progresses (Quote sent -> Booked -> In Repair -> Completed), simply **drag and drop** the card to the next column. 

**Step 3: Follow-up Reminders**
If a customer says "I'll think about it," go to the **Reminders** page and set a date for a follow-up call. The system will track it. Once you call them, click **"✓ Mark Done"** to clear it from the active list.

**Step 4: Email the Customer**
When the car is ready, click the **📧 Email** button on the car's card in the Pipeline. Select the "Car Ready for Pickup" template, click send, and the system will log the email in the **Email Logs** page.

## 📊 Monitoring Your Business
Check the **Dashboard** daily to see:
- **Total Revenue:** Money from completed jobs.
- **Active Pipeline Value:** Estimated money currently in the workshop.
- **Pending Reminders:** Calls your staff needs to make today.
